# Privacy policy: CVAoS Routing Annotator

Last updated: November 10, 2026

CVAoS Routing Annotator is a tool for recording which movement abilities let the player reach each
enemy in Castlevania: Aria of Sorrow. It fills in the logic spreadsheet used by the Archipelago
randomizer for that game. It runs entirely on your own computer.

## What the app can access

When you sign in with Google, the app asks for permission to use files that you open with it. You
then choose the logic spreadsheet in Google's file picker. That spreadsheet is the only file the
app can read or change. It can't see or change any of your other files.

The app reads the spreadsheet's enemy tab. It writes the routing results you record, with the
tester name you enter and the date.

## What the app stores

These are saved only on your computer, in the folder `%LOCALAPPDATA%\cvaos-annotator\`:
- your Google sign-in token;
- your settings;
- a record of your test runs;
- copies of game rooms drawn from your own game file.

None of this is sent to the developer or to anyone else. The app has no server of its own and
collects no analytics.

## What becomes visible to others

What you write to the spreadsheet is visible to everyone who can view it, like any other edit to
that sheet. Google's version history for the spreadsheet shows your edits under your Google
account.

## Use of Google user data

The app uses data from Google APIs only to read and write the spreadsheet you chose, as described
above. Its use of that data follows the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.

## Removing access

You can remove the app's access at any time at
[myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). Deleting the folder
above removes everything the app saved on your computer.
