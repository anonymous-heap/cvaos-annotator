# CVAoS Routing Annotator

A tool for recording which movement abilities let the player reach each enemy in Castlevania: Aria
of Sorrow. The results go into the logic spreadsheet used by the game's Archipelago randomizer. The
tool runs on your own computer, alongside the BizHawk emulator.
